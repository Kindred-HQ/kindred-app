🕊️ Kindred — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 17 October 2026

📌 In Short
Your conversations are yours. We don't sell them. We don't read them. We don't use them for advertising. Period.

1. Who We Are

Kindred is an AI-powered supportive conversation space created and operated by Kindred. We are based in South Africa.

Contact: kindred.hello@proton.me

2. What Information We Collect

We collect as little as possible:

• What you type: The messages you share in conversation

• Session data: Temporary information needed to keep the chat working properly

• No account required: You do not need to give us your name, email address, or any personal details to use Kindred

3. How We Use Your Information

• ✅ Only to respond to you — your messages are processed to generate a helpful, thoughtful reply

• ✅ Temporary processing only — your conversation is sent to our AI service provider solely to generate that response

• ❌ We never sell your data — to anyone, ever

• ❌ We never use your conversations for advertising — no exceptions

• ❌ We never read your conversations — no human at Kindred accesses what you share

Our AI Service Provider

Currently, we use OpenAI's API to process messages. Please note:

• OpenAI processes your message only to generate a response

• We have confirmed that API inputs and outputs are NOT used for OpenAI's model training

• Conversations are processed temporarily and are not permanently stored by us or by OpenAI beyond what is needed

4. Your Rights (POPIA — South Africa)

As a user in South Africa, you have rights under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA):

• ✅ Right to know what data we hold about you

• ✅ Right to request deletion of your data

• ✅ Right to withdraw consent — simply stop using the service

• ✅ Right to complain — if you believe we've mishandled your data, you can report to the South African Information Regulator

Because we don't require accounts or permanently store your personal details, simply closing or clearing your chat removes your conversation from your local session immediately

5. Important Disclaimer

Kindred is NOT a mental health service, medical provider, or crisis hotline. It is a supportive conversation space only:

• ⚠️ Not a substitute for professional care — if you're struggling, please reach out to a qualified professional

• 📞 South African support resources:

◦ SADAG (Depression & Anxiety): 0800 567 567 / 0800 456 789

◦ Lifeline South Africa: 0861 322 322

◦ Suicide Crisis Line: 0800 567 567

6. Children & Minors

Kindred is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we ever update how we handle data, those changes will be reflected here with an updated "Last Updated" date. Significant changes will be communicated clearly within the service.

8. Questions

Have questions about your privacy? We'd love to hear from you:

📧 Email: kindred.hello@proton.me
