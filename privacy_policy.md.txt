🕊️ Kindred — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 22 October 2026

📌 In Short
We do not sell conversation data or use it for advertising. Chat messages are processed to generate replies and are not saved as conversation history in our Supabase database.

1. Who We Are

Kindred is an AI-powered supportive conversation space created and operated by Kindred. We are based in South Africa.

Contact: kindred.hello@proton.me

2. What Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Kindred, the service may process or store:

• Conversation content: Messages are held in the active app session and sent to Amazon Bedrock to generate replies. The app does not currently save chat history to Supabase.

• Account data: If you create an account, Supabase Auth handles your email and authentication. The app's profile record may include premium access status and your selected Kindred Spirit.

• Daily usage data: Supabase stores a daily message count and an identifier used to enforce free limits. For signed-in users this is based on the account ID. For guests, the app derives a pseudonymous identifier from the connection IP using a server-side secret; it does not store the raw IP in the usage table. Guest limits may be affected by shared networks or changes to your connection IP, so guest use is not fully anonymous.

• Voice input: Listening mode is off until you turn it on. While it is on and the app tab is active, your browser's speech-recognition feature listens for the wake phrase and converts your speech to text. Processing of the audio may be subject to your browser or device provider's terms. Kindred does not store raw microphone audio; the resulting transcript is sent to Amazon Bedrock like typed messages. Listening stops when you turn it off or the app tab becomes inactive.

3. How We Use Your Information

• ✅ To provide the service — conversation content is sent to Amazon Bedrock to generate a reply

• ✅ To enforce free limits — daily usage identifiers and counts are stored in Supabase

• ✅ To provide accounts and profile features — account and profile data are handled through Supabase

• ❌ We do not sell your data or use conversations for advertising

The app does not currently save conversations as chat history in Supabase. Messages remain in the active Streamlit session while it is running. The app does not provide an operator-facing conversation-history feature. Service providers process data under their own applicable terms and policies.

Our AI Service Provider

The deployed app currently uses Amazon Bedrock to process chat and whisper prompts. Please review AWS's applicable service terms and privacy information:

• Your message is sent to AWS to generate a response

• We do not make claims here about AWS processing, retention, or model-training practices; those are governed by AWS's current terms and configuration

The separate local development app connects to an Ollama server at localhost. It is not the deployed Bedrock app; its data handling depends on the local Ollama setup.

4. Your Rights (POPIA — South Africa)

As a user in South Africa, you have rights under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA):

• ✅ Right to know what data we hold about you

• ✅ Right to request deletion of your data

• ✅ Right to withdraw consent — simply stop using the service

• ✅ Right to complain — if you believe we've mishandled your data, you can report to the South African Information Regulator

Chat content is not currently saved as persistent conversation history. Account, profile, and daily usage records are separate and are not removed by closing a chat. To ask about or request deletion of account-related records, contact us at the email below. We will handle requests subject to applicable law and operational requirements.

5. Important Disclaimer

6. Children & Minors

Kindred is intended for users aged 13 and older. The app currently includes a Kids Mode option for eligible premium accounts, but it does not include a parent/guardian setup flow. Children should use the app only with a parent or guardian's involvement. Do not provide a child's identifying information.
